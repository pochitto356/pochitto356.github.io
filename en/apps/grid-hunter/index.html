Twin-stick arcade shooter

GRID HUNTER

Rule the grid.

View on the App Store

An endless arcade shooter on a screen-filling grid. Move with your left thumb, aim with your right, and survive wave after wave. A boss shows up every five waves.

Features

  • Twin-stick touch controls: left to move, right to aim. Bullets fire automatically
  • Three bosses with distinct attack patterns (ORBITRON / BLOCK CORE / CHAOS MATRIX), one every five waves
  • Hit a 50-combo to trigger GRID BREAK and clear the board
  • Timed power-ups: bouncing shots, a piercing laser and orbiting blades
  • 18 achievements and score attack
  • Landscape only. iPhone and iPad

Pricing

Free to play with a banner ad. A one-time purchase removes ads. An optional rewarded ad lets you continue after game over.
