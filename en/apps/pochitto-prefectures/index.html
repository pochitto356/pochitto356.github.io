A daily Japan map quiz

Pochitto Prefectures

Which prefecture is lit up on the map?

View on the App Store

Name the Japanese prefecture from a highlighted map, its shape or a clue about what it is famous for. Five new questions every day, and each answer shows the capital and a few facts, so you learn all 47 a minute at a time.

Features

  • Three question types: the prefecture lit up on the map, its silhouette, or a hint
  • Five new questions every midnight: three with 4 choices and two with 6, where neighbors make it trickier
  • Each answer shows the capital, region, local specialties and trivia. Share your result as colored squares, spoiler-free
  • Practice mode with three lives, plus a prefecture collection to complete region by region
  • Streaks, a calendar of past quizzes, 13 badges and a daily reminder. No login

Pricing

Free with ads. A one-time purchase (¥300) removes ads and unlocks unlimited hints.
