Pochitto Yoji

Which kanji fills the gap?

Coming soon

Pick the kanji that completes a Japanese four-character idiom. Five new puzzles every day, and each answer shows the reading and meaning, so you learn as you play.

Features

  • Five new puzzles every midnight, from easy to hard
  • See the reading and meaning after every answer. Share your result as colored squares, spoiler-free
  • Practice mode: three lives, how many in a row can you get?
  • An idiom collection that fills up as you play (296 idioms)
  • Streaks, a calendar of past puzzles and 12 badges. No login

Pricing

Free with ads. A one-time purchase (¥300) removes ads and unlocks unlimited hints.
