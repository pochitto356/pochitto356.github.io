Don't Push the Button

You were told not to. You want to anyway.

View on the App Store

A big red button in the middle of the screen says DON'T PUSH. Tap only the safe buttons and avoid the forbidden one. Each round lasts three seconds.

Features

  • Three-second rounds. Playable in a spare minute
  • The forbidden button keeps moving. Reflexes versus self-control
  • Scores are saved on your device. No account needed
  • Works offline

Pricing

Free with ads. A one-time purchase removes them.
