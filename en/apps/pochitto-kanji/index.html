Pochitto Kanji

Which kanji sits in the middle?

View on the App Store

Four compound words point inward. Find the single kanji they all share — for 学□・□庭・母□・□長 the answer is 校. Three fresh puzzles arrive every midnight and take about three minutes.

Features

  • Three new puzzles every midnight: easy, normal and hard
  • Solve all three to grow your streak. Missed a day? Catch up from the calendar
  • Stuck? Reveal candidate kanji or the readings of the compounds
  • Practice mode with every past puzzle: 469 in total, all everyday jōyō kanji
  • No account. Your records stay on your device

Pricing

Free with ads. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks unlimited hints.
