Pochitto Reflex

Tap the moment it turns green.

Coming soon

Find out your reflex age. Four 30-second tests measure reaction speed, judgment and focus.

Features

  • Reaction test: tap the instant red turns green. Your average of five tries, in milliseconds
  • Don't-push test: tap PUSH right away, never touch DON'T PUSH
  • Color-word test: answer with the ink color, not the word (a Stroop test)
  • Number touch: tap 1 to 25 in order to measure your field of view and focus
  • SS to D ranks, personal bests, a history graph and 12 badges. Works offline

Results include your device's display and touch latency. They are for fun, not a medical test.

Pricing

Free with ads. A one-time purchase (¥300) removes them.
