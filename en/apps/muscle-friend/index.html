Idle muscle-growing game

Muscle Friend

Tap, idle, get swole.

View on the App Store

Tap to train. Muscles keep growing while you're away. Dress-up, gacha and rebirth in a silly idle game. Works offline.

Features

  • Tap to train, or leave it idle and come back stronger
  • Dress-up and gacha for your muscle friend
  • Rebirth system: each run makes you stronger
  • Offline play. No account needed

Pricing

Free to play with seven consumable in-app purchases. An optional rewarded video can be watched up to three times a day. No forced ads.
