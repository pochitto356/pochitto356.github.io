About the developer

pochitto games is a one-person studio run by Kota Konishi, making small iPhone games. The first apps went live on the App Store in August 2026.

"Pochitto" is the Japanese sound of a light tap. The games are built for the minute you wait for a train or the three minutes the kettle takes: a little funny, a little frustrating, and over before you know it.

There is no company behind this, just one person doing the design, code, store submissions and support. If a reply is slow, a game is probably being built.

How the apps are made

  • No personal data is collected. Scores and saves stay on your device
  • Apps with ads always offer a one-time purchase to remove them
  • Every game can be finished without paying
  • Bug reports get a reply as fast as one person can manage
