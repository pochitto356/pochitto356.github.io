Support

Support for pochitto games apps. Please check the FAQ first.

FAQ

How do I remove ads?

Buy "Remove Ads" inside the app (one-time purchase). If ads are still showing after purchase, tap "Restore Purchases" on the same screen.

Can I transfer my score or save data to a new phone?

Scores and saves are stored only on your device, so they cannot be transferred. One-time purchases such as "Remove Ads" can be restored on a new device with the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchases".

I paid but nothing changed.

Close the app fully, reopen it, and tap "Restore Purchases". If that doesn't help, email us with the app name and the time of purchase.

Where are the gacha rates?

Muscle Friend shows the rates on the gacha screen.

I found a bug.

Please email the app name, your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.
